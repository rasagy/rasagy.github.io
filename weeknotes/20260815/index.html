Week #3: Self-fulfilling prophecy, core memories, lost notes

Posted on: 22 Aug, 2026

Aside

(looking at you, dear reader)

I was afraid that like many daily projects and past journaling attempts, I’ll not be able to keep up with writing weeknotes. Well, well, guess what. I failed on the third week itself. Sigh.

SIGH.

(stops looking at you, and goes back to narrating typing my life)

Weeknotes

  • Saturday began with my Ultimate frisbee training early in the morning. We learnt about Defense, specifically focusing on strategies to prioritize which areas you want to guard vs nudge the offense towards. I really enjoyed the visual explanations about Zones as quarters around you, and then deciding the most important ones based on the force. It’s so fascinating how simple visual explanations can explain complex game mechanics!
  • Next up was IndieWebClub meetup, where I posted my first two weeknotes, and lamented about how I have never been able to be consistent with regular activities like these. Dear Reader: I do know myself too well.
  • Part of the reason I couldn’t finish this weeknote was what happened next. If you know me well enough, you know how much I love my sleep. No matter whether I’m at parties, or even when I have friends over at my place, I drift away by 10pm/11pm (with occasional sparks of complete consciousness & full attention that always amuses my friends). Today, one thing led to another, and I found myself in the company of a few folks (that I’ve known of, but never hung out with), and evening cafe run continued to dinner, to hanging at one of their places. The conversations flew so naturally — often deep, personal & thoughtful — I didn’t notice time pass by. Time did pass by fly by, and it was only after I went back home post-breakfast that I realized we had spent 15+ hours talking.
  • I don’t think I’ll ever be able to really articulate how I felt this weekend, apart from it becoming a core memory.
  • While this may not sound as surprising to others, I have never done this. EVER. I still don’t quite know how this happened. I’ve been recently practicing saying yes especially for things outside my comfort zone — thanks to Improvlore & Jim Carrey’s Yes Man — only to discover that there is often joy in redefining what is comfortable to me. It was also perhaps the kind of conversations that I was really yearning for, and a form of closeness & vulnerability that I had not felt in a really long time. I have now discovered that I can, in fact, trade my sleep for some wonderful conversations. As my mom says, देर आए दुरुस्त आए (Better late than never).
  • I missed a furniture delivery on Sunday morning because my phone was on mute all this while, and after profusely apologizing and giving a generous tip, I got the bookshelf I’ve been eyeing. You can not build a nice home without having a nice home for your books.
  • I do not remember much more from the weekend, apart from feeling very grateful to have spent time with these wonderful folks. (If you were there and are reading this, I’m forever grateful to you for this!)
  • I started teaching my module on Tangible Data Visualization at NID Bangalore. This is the fifth edition of this module, and I kicked off the class with examples of what the past batches have designed, and an ambitious three week timeline for us to try to follow. Balancing office work with teaching is always difficult, but the joy of watching students discover what data visualization can be has always been worth it.
  • By the end of this week, I had fragments of these notes put together, only for my Obsidian Git sync to fall apart. And it fell apart in the most frustrating way possible: one of my files had a unicode character that led to a syncing issue on my iPhone, and it took me weeks of trying to fix this. Eventually this weeknote and a few other notes were purged from my git history (is that a thing?) and I got too lazy to write this weeknote again. Weeks turned to months, life moved on as it does. Until months later, when I was offered a choice to forget these weeks and restart. These weeks shall not be forgotten, so I am rewriting this in November. You know, just like how my mom says, देर आए दुरुस्त आए (Better late than never).
  • However: I will cheat on the post date to keep the order consistent. Which means these posts shall arrive non-linearly. Sorry.
  • And with this, I do NOT declare bankruptcy!
