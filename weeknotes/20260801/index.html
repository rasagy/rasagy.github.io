Week #1: Training, Friendships & Community

Posted on: 8 Aug, 2026

Intro: Why write weeknotes?

I recently lamented that half of 2026 was over, and it’s personally been the hardest year for me. And by force of habit, I asked myself, “What else is true?”.

I tend to obsess over big challenges & what’s not going right with life, and often forget to acknowledge what is going right. This is also why my past attempts at journaling just spiral into negativity. This is an attempt to force myself to break out of this loop, and celebrate the small joys of everyday life.

Weeknotes

  • August started with two impromptu decisions that I was excited about. First: I’ve been playing Ultimate Frisbee for a few years now, but only recreationally. So SU suggested I sign up for more structured training sessions to get better. I’ll now spend 6 Saturdays, waking up before 6am, to learn how to run around & throw a disc. I’m very excited!
  • First Frisbee training session kicked off with a focus on improving throws. Having a few familiar faces helped. I focused on improving the grip for my backhand throw, doing pump fakes, and really using my wrists. There is a different kind of joy in learning something with a structure & a coach.
  • My second impromptu decision was to make a last minute plan to fly to Mumbai for celebrating SB’s birthday. I went with very little planning, very little packing, and picked a hotel to stay at on my way to the airport. I hope I can get used to travelling like this and not be anxious about every small thing that might go wrong.
  • I made a small photo-zine from my Japan trip, as a gift for SB who is heading to Japan this weekend. Making photo-zines has become my favorite kind of gift for special birthdays — it makes me revisit photos and gives them a more tangible existence, and most of my friends prefer hand-crafted things over purchased items.
  • SB did three parties (we joke about them being cohorts!) to celebrate, and I was just in awe of how their social circle is filled with incredibly kind people. I’ve always admired how SB constantly thinks of their friends and puts efforts in maintaining these relationships, so it was even more inspiring to see this in person. I’m glad I made the trip for this!
  • I fell sick by the end of Saturday — sleep exhaustion, weather change, and too much AC all added up I guess. Thankfully, I didn’t spend the next two days being sick alone in a hotel, and instead went over to AM’s home who offered delicious home-made khichdi & a bed to crash in. So grateful to have friends in different cities who would take care of me!
  • On my way to the airport, I went to IDC, IIT Bombay to meet two of my favorite design professors. I really miss how time feels frozen here — so much feels just like it was a decade back. I also love how deeply thoughtful every conversation is. I miss my annual ritual to teach here, and thankfully my prof found another topic for me to come back and teach.
  • Leisurely arrived at the airport 6 hours before my flight, which is too early even by my standards. I did some reading, some doomscrolling, had expensive yet average chai, and had an overall slow evening.
  • Spent the rest of the week slowly recovering. A lot of tomato soup, curd rice & khichdi was consumed.
  • Pushed myself to go for Improvlore’s Whimsical Wednesday jam. Played a bunch of small games, all focused on helping me not overthink & learn to respond naturally. Felt much better then sitting alone at home.
  • Met PR for dinner, and realised the most important part of being a friend is to be honest: to call out when they are avoiding facing the truth or stuck in thought loops. Grateful that PR did that for me.
  • Spent most of my Friday at a workshop by a non-profit, brainstorming how to tell better data stories. It was really nice to be in a room filled with young, passionate information designers & techies. I reminisced Global Service (Design) Jam & DataKind Sprints, and hoped that we build more communities where we do meaningful things (not just talk).
  • Despite having so many unread books at home — along with an ever growing TBR list on goodreads — I bought 5 more books from Blossoms. Clearly collecting books is a different hobby than reading them.
  • Decided to start writing weeknotes!
